Privacy Policy

Bunny Momos staff app · last updated 28 October 2026

Bunny Momos runs a momo shop and a production kitchen in West Bengal, India. This app is used only by our own staff and the owner to run the business: sales, stock, production, expenses, attendance and workplace photo checks. It is not offered to the public.

What the app keeps

  • Business records our staff enter: sales, stock, production, expenses and cash counts.
  • Staff details needed to run the team: name, role, workplace, shift times, leave and attendance check-ins.
  • Photos staff take of their work areas for daily cleanliness checks.

These are stored on our app server and used only to run Bunny Momos. We do not sell them, show ads, or share them with anyone outside the business.

Google Drive

The owner can connect their own Google Drive so the app can keep a nightly backup of the records and keep the workplace photos. For this the app asks Google only for the drive.file permission, which lets it see and change only the files and folders it creates itself in a "Bunny Momos" folder. It cannot see, read or change anything else in that Drive.

Backups are encrypted before they are uploaded. Data from Google is used only to store and read back these backup files and photos. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The owner can disconnect the app at any time from their Google Account under Security, "Third-party apps with account access". The files already in the Bunny Momos folder stay theirs.

AI checks

Some checks, such as photo cleanliness scores and spotting unusual entries, use an AI service (Anthropic's Claude). Only what a check needs is sent, such as the photo or the figures being checked; phone numbers, ID documents and salaries are never sent. The AI provider does not use this data to train its models.

Keeping and removing data

Records are kept while they are needed for the business and for the law. A staff member can ask the owner to see or correct their details, or to remove them when they leave.

Questions

Staff can ask the owner directly or through the app. Anyone else can ask at our store.
